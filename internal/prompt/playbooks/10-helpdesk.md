The helpdesk thread is the customer's own account of the problem, and the record of what
L1 already told them before this ticket reached you.

## How to use it

- Read the whole thread, not just the last message. Earlier messages often carry detail that
  later summaries drop.
- Note what L1 already told the customer, and whether that guidance was correct. Repeating
  wrong guidance is worse than staying silent.
- Screenshots and attachments are evidence, not decoration. Look at the URL bar, the timestamp
  shown on screen, and any error text visible in the image before treating a screenshot as
  confirming a hypothesis.
- Match the customer's account against what the evidence sources actually show; do not assume
  the customer's framing of the problem is the same as its cause.
